/*How to determine modality by looking at the schema:

    For a 1:1 relationship, both sides must be are required. 
    For a 1:M relationship, look at the foreign key on the MANY side:
        If it has the `NOT NULL` constraint, then the `1` side is required.
        Otherwise, the `1` side is optional.
        The MANY side in both cases is optional.
    For a M:M relationship, look at the foreign keys in  the join table.
        If one of the keys is `NOT NULL`, then the referenced table is required.
        Otherwise, the referenced table is optional.
